Executive Summary

Private Investment Tsavo Line

The Opportunity

Terraquest Investments - FZCO ("Terraquest") invites Epiidosis Investments LLC ("Epiidosis") to co-fund a private $100,000,000 Secured Revolving Trade Credit Facility under the proprietary Tsavo Trade Credit Line in support of an executed total processing contract.

Processing Contract

The facility finances and monetizes an executed commodity processing contract: bulk elemental Sulphur procurement from Abu Dhabi National Oil Company (ADNOC), importation into South Africa via Natpower SA, toll-conversion into industrial Sulphuric Acid (H2SO4) at Foskor (Pty) Ltd's Richards Bay complex under an executed tolling contract, and pre-paid liquidation into high-demand hydrometallurgical mining basins across the SADC Copperbelt.

Credit Security

The credit structure is self-liquidating, senior-secured, and bankruptcy-remote. Capital circulates through a closed-loop settlement architecture backed by continuous possessory title, on-site collateral management, and a strict 100% pre-paid cash collection protocol.

Credit Architecture

Closed-Loop Structure Balance Isolation

Structural Isolation

Ring-Fenced Balance
  • Capital deployment is strictly asset-backed and ring-fenced from Natpower's general operating balance sheet.
  • Capital flows strictly between prime issuing banks, sovereign suppliers, bonded logistics handlers, and controlled collection escrows.
  • Dual signatory governance ensures non-commingled, bankruptcy-remote fund routing at every stage.
Zero Contagion

Collateral Coverage

Asset Floor
  • Senior possessory title maintained over physical assets at every operational node: marine transit, port handling, work-in-progress, and finished acid inventory.
  • Under contractually fixed transformation yields (1 MT Sulphur → 1.438 MT Net Acid), intrinsic collateral coverage maintains a minimum 120% asset-to-debt valuation.
  • Continuous physical cushion insulates capital partners against benchmark commodity price swings.
120% Coverage

Custody Control

Manifold Lock-Out
  • Accredited third-party inspection agency holds tripartite Collateral Management Agreement (CMA) with physical lock-out authority over storage manifolds.
  • Zero uncollateralized credit: product discharge valves open only against irrevocably cleared cash deposits in the escrow account.
  • Daily volumetric audits and plant intake surveillance protect against inventory leakage.

Net Retained Yield: 1.438 MT Net Acid per MT raw Sulphur (47%)

Cost Recovery

Foskor recovers its toll processing and plant operational expenditures by retaining a contractually stipulated 53% in-kind volume of the finished Sulphuric Acid. This in-kind recovery model fully shields capital partners from plant overhead, labor, electricity, catalyst consumption, and maintenance risks.
