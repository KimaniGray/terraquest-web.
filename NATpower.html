Risk Mitigation

Section 07 Underwriting Defenses
Risk Category Identified Vulnerability Institutional Defensive Mitigation
Price Volatility Sudden adverse fluctuations in benchmark crude / refined crack spreads. 120% asset collateral cushion, short 60-day turnover cycles, plus hedging overlays on crack spread.
Counterparty Default Off-taker insolvency or refusal to accept refined product deliveries. 100% cash prepayment or irrevocable documentary bank guarantee required prior to tank release.
Leakage / Theft Unauthorized diversion or inventory shrinkage at port or storage tanks. Tier-1 international Collateral Management Agreement (CMA); physical manifold locks and continuous SGS flow metering.
Operational Breakdown Refinery downtime or maintenance bottlenecks during tolling phase. Pre-inspected Tier-1 refinery contracts with liquidated delay damages and alternative tolling capacity agreements.
Marine & Transit Vessel casualty, piracy, demurrage delays, or maritime contamination. Full marine transit insurance (Institute Cargo Clauses A 110%) underwritten by AA-rated European syndicates.
Sovereign / FX Risk Local currency devaluations or central bank repatriation controls. UAE (ADGM / English Law jurisdiction, USD settlement) and South Africa (SARS customs compliance, bonded port transit corridor). Zero local FX exposure; all trade operations settled strictly in USD hard currency.
