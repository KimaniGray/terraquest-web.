Executive Summary

Trade Line

Funding Invitation

Terraquest Investments - FZCO ("Terraquest") invites Epiidosis Investments LLC ("Epiidosis") to co-fund an institutional $100,000,000 Secured Revolving Trade Credit Facility under the proprietary Tsavo Trade Credit Line.

Transformation Arbitrage

The facility monetizes an executed commodity transformation arbitrage: bulk elemental Sulphur procurement from Abu Dhabi National Oil Company (ADNOC), importation into South Africa via Natpower SA, toll-conversion into industrial Sulphuric Acid (H2SO4) at Foskor (Pty) Ltd’s Richards Bay complex under an executed tolling contract, and pre-paid liquidation into high-demand hydrometallurgical mining basins across the SADC Copperbelt.

Credit Security

The credit structure is self-liquidating, senior-secured, and bankruptcy-remote. Capital circulates through a closed-loop settlement architecture backed by continuous possessory title, on-site collateral management, and a strict 100% pre-paid cash collection protocol.

Conversion Economics

Tolling Parameters

Tolling Contract

Under the executed Foskor Tolling Agreement, product economics and volumetric yields are contractually fixed:

  • Conversion Ratio: 1 MT Sulphur (S) → 3.06 MT Sulphuric Acid (H2SO4) gross industrial yield.
  • Net Retained Yield: 1.438 MT H2SO4 per metric ton of raw Sulphur processed (47% retained acid volume after 53% tolling fee).
  • Tranche Output: 71,910 MT of net commercial H2SO4 contractually guaranteed under warehouse receipt per 50,000 MT raw delivery.
  • Annualized Asset: 431,460 MT of net retained, monetizable H2SO4.

Cost Transfer

Foskor absorbs all direct conversion overhead, power costs, catalyst consumption, plant maintenance, and labor within its 53% split, eliminating manufacturing cost risk for the capital partners.
