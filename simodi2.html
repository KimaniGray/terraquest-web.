Section 3: Technical Circuit (1 MT / Week Capacity)
SHEET 03 / 08

Key Equipment and Process Flows

Engineered for 1 Metric Ton per operating week (200 kg / day) across four interconnected metallurgical stations.

STEP 01 Intake & Verification

Receiving, Assay & Settlement

Instant non-destructive analysis for spot cash settlement backed by wet fire assay.

  • • Benchtop ED-XRF: 60-second non-destructive purity test.
  • • Fire Assay Cupellation: Lead fusion gravimetric testing.
  • • ICP-OES Spectrometer: Trace element purity verification.
  • • OECD Traceability: Barcoded intake batch logging.
STEP 02 Pre-Treatment

Melting, Granulation & Abatement

Thermal homogenization and graining for optimal chemical digestion surface area.

  • • Induction Furnaces (x2): 75 kg tilting units (1,150°C).
  • • Water Granulation Tank: High-pressure 2-4mm corn.
  • • Mercury Abatement: Chilled vapor traps & carbon filters.
  • • Closed Retort System: Eliminates atmospheric mercury.
STEP 03 Refining Circuit

Chemical Refining & Neutralization

Acid dissolution and selective SMBS precipitation yielding 99.9% pure gold sponge.

  • • Aqua Regia Reactors (x3): 800L jacketed PTFE units.
  • • SMBS Precipitation: Selective fine gold recovery.
  • • Multi-Stage Scrubbers: Caustic NOx acid scrubbers.
  • • ETP Plant: Heavy metal precipitation & filter press.
STEP 04 Minting & Finishing

Casting, Stamping & Vaulting

Production of certified stamped investment bars, cast ingots, and secure storage.

  • • Pure Gold Furnaces (x2): 50 kg dedicated melt.
  • • 400-Ton Minting Press: High-relief mirror finish dies.
  • • Fiber Laser Engraver: Unique serial number marking.
  • • Class Modular Vault: 3 MT capacity dual custody.

Section 6: Institutional Alignment & Strategic Case
SHEET 06 / 08

Strategic Rationale: Why This Partnership Is Necessary

Neither private capital nor the state can unlock the precious metals sector independently.

Why the Government Needs the Operator

USD 30M Private Capital Commitment:

NorthRock funds the entire industrial facility and liquidity reserve without state debt or drawing on public reserves.

USD 25M Liquidity Captures Artisanal Gold:

Raw gold leaks across informal borders primarily because miners require immediate cash; instant spot payouts formalize the supply.

Industrial Technology & DMCC Market Access:

Operator supplies 800L chemical digestion circuits, 400T minting press, ICP-OES certification, and off-take channels into Dubai.

OECD Supply Chain Governance:

Embeds international traceability standards that give Somaliland bullion international market acceptance.

Why the Operator Needs the Government

25-Year Operating Exclusivity:

Processing 1 MT/week requires predictable rules; the state guarantees investment security via Presidential Decree.

Mandatory Domestic Refining Policy:

State regulations requiring all domestic gold to be refined before export ensure consistent feed for the 50 MT annual capacity.

Artisanal Formalization & Regional Logistics:

Ministry organizes miners into legal cooperatives and integrates them into formal regional refinery collection depots.

Sovereign Security & Transport Escorts:

Coordinated state security escorting bullion consignments between mining belts, Hargeisa, and Egal International Airport.

National Macroeconomic Impact:

Value Retention:

Keeps refining margins, minting value-add, and processing revenues inside the domestic economy.

Forex Reserves:

Official export revenues clear through the Central Bank, strengthening national foreign exchange liquidity.

Regional Leadership:

Positions Hargeisa as the certified precious metals refining hub across the entire Horn of Africa.
