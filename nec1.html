CRITICAL REGULATORY NOTE: MULTI-REGULATOR ENGAGEMENT STRATEGY

Because the ACIP data sets bridge finance, insurance, and agriculture, commercial deployment requires strict compliance. Post-close of the initial scope, TerraQuest will facilitate structured engagements with the necessary regulatory bodies before any live PoC launch:

  • • CBK & SASRA: To clear alternative data usage for credit scoring and closed-loop disbursements for Banks, MFBs, and SACCOs.
  • • IRA (Insurance Regulatory Authority): To ensure CropScope's parametric triggers meet the Insurance Regulatory Authority's compliance standards.
  • • Ministry of Co-operatives: To validate VunaPay check-off integrations with agricultural cooperatives.
  • • ODPC (Office of the Data Protection Commissioner): A mandatory clearance to ensure the cross-pollination of Apollo credit data and VunaPay digital registries complies with Kenya's stringent Data Protection Act.
Outcome: Contracted Pilots, Live ACIP Deployment & Regulatory Clearance
