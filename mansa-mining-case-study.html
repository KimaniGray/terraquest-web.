01 The Transaction Puzzle

The Challenge: Isolated CAPEX

Mansa Mining SARLU has secured a lucrative transport contract with Zhicheng Guinée Mining SARL to haul bauxite. However, executing this contract requires significant upfront capital to acquire an initial fleet of 100 trucks. Securing a standalone $9.5M asset finance facility locally presents significant balance sheet risk and high collateral demands.

Transaction 1: The Contract (CAPEX)

  • Operator: Mansa Mining SARLU
  • Client: Zhicheng Guinée Mining SARL
  • Task: Bauxite transport
  • Fleet Requirement: 100 Trucks (Initial Phase of 300 target)
  • CAPEX Required: $9,500,000 USD

The Tsavo Solution: Embedded Financing

To unlock the $9.5M CAPEX seamlessly, Tsavo removes the focus from Mansa Mining's local balance sheet and shifts the credit view to the end-buyer of the bauxite. By embedding Transaction 1 (Logistics CAPEX) inside Transaction 2 (Bauxite Export), we fund the initial 100 trucks using the ultimate Off-Taker's global credit rating.

Transaction 2: The Trade Support

  • Asset: Bauxite Export Contract
  • Off-Taker (Buyer): Global Commodity Buyer
  • Instrument: Standby Letter of Credit (SBLC)
  • Mechanic: SBLC is issued to Zebu, discounted, and routed by TerraQuest specifically to pay the truck manufacturers.

03 Roles & Bank Caveats

Party Responsibilities

Mansa Mining (Operator)

Executes the transport mandate, hauling bauxite from the Zhicheng mine to Kimbo port, fulfilling the physical performance requirement of the underlying trade.

TerraQuest (Deal Checker)

A specific verification office that reviews all performance and payment documents for settlement. TerraQuest clears the documents and presents them to the Trade Agent to trigger execution.

Zebu (Trade Agent)

Receives the SBLC from the Off-Taker's bank, processes the discounting mechanism, and deploys the capital based solely on the Deal Checker's clearance.

SBLC Bank Caveats & Format

To successfully fund the Mansa Mining CAPEX through the Off-Taker, the financial instrument must meet strict institutional criteria:

Eligible Issuing Banks

The SBLC must be issued or fully confirmed by a Top 50 Global Bank located in an OECD jurisdiction. Local or unrated regional banks cannot be monetized for this facility.

Instrument Formatting

Must be transmitted via SWIFT MT760 (Standby Letter of Credit) or MT700 (Documentary LC). It must explicitly state it is subject to UCP 600 or ISP98.

Transferability

The instrument must be Irrevocable, Unconditional, and Transferable/Assignable to allow Zebu to establish the discounting line.
