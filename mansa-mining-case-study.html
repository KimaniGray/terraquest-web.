01 The Transaction Puzzle

The Challenge: Isolated CAPEX

Mansa Mining SARLU currently holds a transport contract paying $10/MT to haul bauxite. The 2026 starting requirement is a $9,500,000 USD CAPEX facility to acquire an initial fleet of 100 trucks. This is Phase 1 of a larger objective to scale to a target fleet of 300 trucks by 2028.

"We do not believe this standalone transport contract will suffice to secure the required funding."

The Hurdle (Transaction 1)

Securing a traditional $9.5M asset finance facility locally for Phase 1—based solely on logistics revenue—presents significant balance sheet risk, high collateral demands, and critically restricts the rapid 2027/2028 growth trajectory.

The Tsavo Solution: Structured Trade

To unlock the $9.5M seamlessly, Tsavo proposes a structural shift: elevating Mansa from a pure logistics provider to a Principal Commodity Exporter through a comprehensive structured trade solution.

The Engineered Pivot (Transaction 2)

  • Global Off-Take: Mansa purchases bauxite and exports it directly to a Global Buyer.
  • Bankable Commitment: Secure an acceptable Deferred Letter of Credit (DLC) from this Buyer.
  • Embedded Logistics: Structure the initial $9.5M truck acquisition as a core cost component embedded within the total export trade.
The Financial Engine: The Tsavo Trade Credit Line discounts the Global Buyer's DLC, generating the upfront liquidity to fund the $9.5M CAPEX. This ensures the initial 100 trucks are 100% debt-funded against the institutional credit rating of the Global Off-Taker.

03 Evolved Party Roles

By transitioning to a structured trade model, the responsibilities of the parties are elevated to maintain a secure, closed-loop environment.

Mansa Mining

Principal Exporter

Mansa is no longer just a subcontractor; they are the Principal Seller. They hold the export contract, operate the newly acquired 100-truck fleet, and are responsible for the physical delivery of the bauxite to the Global Buyer.

Global Buyer

Credit Anchor

Provides the bankable commitment (Deferred LC) that serves as the institutional-grade collateral foundation for the entire operation and logistics funding.

TerraQuest

Deal Checker

Acts as the crucial risk-mitigation layer. Ensures discounted funds are not misallocated by clearing all performance documents and strictly routing the $9.5M to the truck supplier, rather than releasing uncontrolled cash.

Zebu Invest.

Capital Provider

The institutional liquidity partner. Zebu receives the DLC, executes the discounting mechanics, and deploys the capital based solely on TerraQuest’s clearance.

04 Economics & Bridging the CAPEX Gap

The primary objective of this structure is to achieve Mansa Mining's goal of fully retiring the $9.5M initial fleet debt within 12 months, enabling cash-funded expansion of the remaining 200 trucks in 2027/2028.

180 Days Discounting Period (Per Tranche)
360 Days Maximum Facility Limit (12 Months)

Strategic Avenues to Bridge the $9.5M Funding

Since a single 180-day bauxite shipment may not generate enough net margin to instantly pay off $9.5M worth of trucks, the CAPEX payoff is integrated into the revolving nature of the trade facility via three options:

Margin Sweeping

The robust net operating margins generated from the FOB/CIF spread of multiple bauxite sales are aggressively swept to pay down the $9.5M principal by the end of Year 1.

Preferred Route

Matched Payments

If the logistics contract remains active alongside the export trade, the $10/MT logistics revenue is combined with export margins to create a dual-revenue liquidation mechanism.

Lease-to-Own

Structure the $9.5M acquisition as a lease-to-own model funded by the trade line. Mansa pays installments from each 180-day cycle, converting to full equity at 12 months.

05 DLC Bank Caveats & Required Verbiage

Because Transaction 1 relies entirely on the creditworthiness of Transaction 2, the financial instrument securing the trade must meet strict institutional criteria.

Eligible Issuing Banks

The DLC must be issued or fully confirmed by a Top 50 Global Bank located in an OECD jurisdiction. Local, unrated, or tier-3 regional banks cannot be monetized for this facility.

Transferability

The instrument must be Irrevocable, Unconditional, and Transferable/Assignable to allow Zebu to legally establish the discounting line.
