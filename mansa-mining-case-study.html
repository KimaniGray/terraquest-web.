01 The Transaction Puzzle

The Challenge: Isolated CAPEX

Mansa Mining SARLU has secured a lucrative transport contract with Zhicheng Guinée Mining SARL to haul bauxite 110km. However, executing this contract requires significant upfront capital to acquire a fleet of 53 trucks. Securing a standalone $5M asset finance facility locally presents significant balance sheet risk and high collateral demands.

Transaction 1: The Contract (CAPEX)

  • Operator: Mansa Mining SARLU
  • Client: Zhicheng Guinée Mining SARL
  • Task: Bauxite transport (110km route)
  • Fleet Requirement: 53 Trucks (Phased delivery: 21 + 32)
  • CAPEX Required: $5,000,000 USD

The Tsavo Solution: Embedded Financing

To unlock the $5M CAPEX seamlessly, Tsavo removes the focus from Mansa Mining's local balance sheet and shifts the credit view to the end-buyer of the bauxite. By embedding Transaction 1 (Logistics CAPEX) inside Transaction 2 (Bauxite Export), we fund the trucks using the ultimate Off-Taker's global credit rating.

Transaction 2: The Trade Support

  • Asset: Bauxite Export Contract
  • Off-Taker (Buyer): Global Commodity Buyer
  • Instrument: Standby Letter of Credit (SBLC)
  • Mechanic: SBLC is issued to Zebu, discounted, and routed by TerraQuest specifically to pay the truck manufacturers.
