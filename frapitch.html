1. Market Context

Under the Food Reserve Act, the FRA must administer the National Strategic Food Reserve. Its legal purpose is to:

  • ■ Ensure reliable supply.
  • ■ Meet local shortfalls.
  • ■ Handle food emergencies.
  • ■ Correct market manipulation.

To meet these goals, the FRA developed a National Maize Program aiming to build a 500k-1M MT strategic reserve and purchase an additional 1M MT for market stabilization.

Requirement: Predictable USD 500M / Year

Currently, the FRA lacks an independent funding mechanism. This severe shortage threatens the program, national food security, and the livelihoods of over 1,000,000 small-scale farmers.

Treasury Dependency

In 2024, the Treasury issued ZMW 2.85 Billion in direct grants to keep the FRA operating. This diverts critical funds away from other national priorities.

Trapped Capital

The FRA routinely releases grain to state bodies during emergencies. Without a pre-funded mechanism, these receivables go unpaid, wiping out working capital for the next season.

Audit Vulnerabilities

Traditional storage lacks certified inventory management. This prevents the State from maintaining a bankable, auditable national reserve.

2. Five-Year Vision

To fund this mandate and permanently relieve the Treasury, TerraQuest Investments will syndicate the USD 400 Million Tsavo Resilience Food Fund 1.

This capital will operate alongside a predictable USD 100 Million contribution from the Government of Zambia.

Built Entirely On Predictability
01

Hard Currency Generation

We bring both the initial USD 400 Million investment and a ready regional export market. This secures USD-denominated contracts for Zambia's surplus maize, creating a predictable foreign exchange earner.

02

Permanent Treasury Relief

Anchoring long-term institutional capital ends the FRA's reliance on erratic government grants. This predictable structure frees billions of Kwacha for national development.

03

Transforming Rural Farming

Guaranteeing year-on-year funding ensures over 1,000,000 small-scale farmers receive prompt payments. This predictability stabilizes rural economies and incentivizes scaled production.

04

Foreign Direct Investment

A fully funded, predictable procurement program acts as a magnet for an additional USD 500 Million in FDI. This capital will modernize logistics, build climate-controlled bulk silos, and deploy agricultural power solutions.
