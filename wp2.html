Context & The Trillion-Shilling Imperative

The Kenyan retirement benefits industry oversees aggregate Assets Under Management (AUM) exceeding KES 2.0 Trillion (~USD 15.5B). Within this universe, Guaranteed Funds (Table G, Category 4 — Approved Issuer or 3rd party guarantees) represent the single fastest-expanding allocation segment, holding KES 597.07 Billion as of June 2026. However, five life insurers control 83.42% (KES 498.07 Billion) through unsegregated Deposit Administration Contracts commingled into general life funds. Pension trustees operate as unsecured creditors exposed to insurer balance sheet risk. A USD 400M (~KES 54.0B) issuance positions Dyer & Blair immediately as the #6 Approved Issuer nationwide, introducing ring-fenced, hard-currency collateral protection.

Current Legal Framework: An Approved Issuer by Law

RETIREMENT BENEFITS REGULATIONS (INTERPRETATION — REGULATION 3)
Verbatim Phrasing
““approved issuer” means an insurer registered under the provisions of the Insurance Act (Cap. 487) or any other issuer approved in writing under the provisions of the Capital Markets Authority Act (Cap. 485A) or under any other written law;”

Under the Current Legal Framework, this governing statutory definition in Kenyan pension jurisprudence establishes three distinct, legally recognized Approved Issuer channels qualifying under RBA Investment Guidelines (Table G, Category 4)—granting retirement benefit schemes statutory authority to allocate up to 100% of total scheme assets:

CHANNEL 1 • IRA REGIME Cap. 487
Registered Life Insurers
• Statutory Text: “an insurer registered under Insurance Act (Cap. 487)”
• Product: Deposit Administration (DA) policies commingled directly into general life funds.
• Statutory Cap: Constrained by IRA Statutory Cap of 4.00% p.a. Guaranteed rate cannot exceed 4%; returns above this are discretionary, smoothed retrospectively, and carry uncollateralized corporate credit risk.
Status: 4.00% Statutory Ceiling
CHANNEL 2 • CMA REGIME Cap. 485A
CMA Approved Issuers
• Statutory Text: “or any other issuer approved in writing under the CMA Act”
• Participating Issuer: Dyer & Blair Investment Bank (CMA Licensed Investment Bank).
• No Statutory Cap: Yield directly aligns to underlying trade asset (14%–15% gross), delivering contractual net return of 10.50%–12.00% USD (14.5%–16% KES) via bankruptcy-remote Sub-SPVs.
Status: No Statutory Cap (Pass-Through)
CHANNEL 3 • CBK REGIME Cap. 488 / Law
Commercial Banks & Guarantees
• Statutory Text: “or under any other written law” (Banking Act, Cap. 488 & statutory bodies).
• Structure: Tier-1 Commercial Bank Guarantees, Standby Letters of Credit (SBLCs), or Multilateral Irrevocable Undertakings.
• No Statutory Cap: Senior credit wrap satisfying Table G Category 4 without being subject to IRA life insurance solvency rules or the 4% DA cap.
Status: Qualifying 3rd Party Wrap

Trustee Ownership

Every Sub-SPV is 100% legally owned by an RBA-licensed Corporate Trustee, creating absolute bankruptcy remoteness from both Dyer & Blair and Terraquest balance sheets.
