STATION 01 Receiving

Intake & Assaying

Doré receipt under triple CCTV surveillance. Immediate weighing on Sartorius 0.01g balances, preliminary ED-XRF scanning, and destructive Cupellation Fire Assay testing.

Tech: ED-XRF • Fire Assay Muffle Furnace • ICP-OES
STATION 02 Pyrometallurgy

Induction & Granulation

Doré bars melted in 50kW IGBT induction furnaces with mercury abatement. Molten gold poured into chilled water granulators creating high surface-area flakes optimized for chemical digestion.

Tech: Chilled Water Granulator • Mercury Scrubber
STATION 03 Hydrometallurgy

Aqua Regia & Refining

Digestion inside enclosed 800L glass-lined chemical reactors (HCl + HNO3). Selective reduction with Sodium Metabisulfite (SMBS) precipitating pure 99.95%+ gold mud with zero emission scrubbers.

Tech: 800L Aqua Regia • SMBS Precipitation • Scrubber
STATION 04 Fabrication

Hydraulic Minting & Vault

Refined gold sponge dried and induction-melted into investment ingots. 400-ton stamping presses create pristine proof-finish minted bars, laser engraved with serialized QR certificates.
