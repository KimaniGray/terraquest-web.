Section 3

Key Equipment and Process Flows

Industrial flow engineered for 1 Metric Ton per week (200 kg/operating day) across four connected stations.

STEP 01 Verification

Receiving, Assay & Verification

Rapid non-destructive testing for daily cash settlement backed by fire assay.

• Benchtop ED-XRF: 60-sec non-destructive purity readout.
• Fire Assay Cupellation: High-temperature lead fusion.
• ICP-OES Spectrometer: Trace element purity verification.
STEP 02 Pre-Treatment

Melting, Granulation & Mercury Abatement

Thermal homogenization and graining for chemical digestion surface area.

• Induction Furnaces (x2): 75 kg tilting units (1,150°C).
• Water Granulation Tank: High-pressure 2–4mm corn.
• Mercury Condenser: Chilled traps & carbon vapor filtration.
STEP 03 Refining

Chemical Refining & Neutralization

Acid dissolution and selective SMBS precipitation to reach 99.9% fine gold.

• Aqua Regia Reactors (x3): 800L jacketed PTFE units.
• SMBS Precipitation: Selective 99.9% sponge recovery.
• Wet Scrubbers & ETP: Caustic NOx neutralization & filter press.
STEP 04 Finishing

Casting, Stamped Bars & Vaulting

Production of certified stamped investment bars and 1 kg wholesale ingots.

• Pure Gold Furnaces (x2): 50 kg fine melt without contamination.
• 400-Ton Minting Press: High-relief mirror dies.
• Laser Engraver & Vault: Alphanumeric serials & 3 MT vault.
Operational Sequence:
Raw Intake (XRF) ➔ Granulation ➔ 800L Aqua Regia ➔ SMBS 99.9% ➔ 400T Minting ➔ Dubai Off-Take

Section 6

Strategic Rationale: Why This Partnership Is Necessary

A public-private alliance is essential to transform Somaliland's informal gold trade into a structured national industry.

🏛️

Why the Government Needs the Operator

  • USD 30M Full Private Capital: NorthRock funds the entire facility and cash buying reserve with zero state debt or burden on sovereign treasury.
  • USD 25M Float Captures Artisanal Gold: Miners sell across informal borders solely because they need immediate cash; spot payouts bring volume into the formal economy.
  • Technical Capability & DMCC Off-Take: Provides 800L chemical refining units, 400T minting press, ICP-OES lab certification, and direct sales clearing into Dubai.
🤝

Why the Operator Needs the Government

  • 25-Year Operating Exclusivity: Long-term regulatory certainty and protection for private capital guaranteed by executive Presidential Decree.
  • Mandatory Domestic Refining Rule: Requires all raw gold to be refined in-country before export, securing continuous supply for the 1 MT/week plant.
  • Cooperative Organization & Security: Line ministry integrates regional miners into legal cooperatives and provides state security transit escorts to the airport.
National Economic Transformation:

The partnership transitions the mineral sector from unrecorded informal trade into certified, hallmarked exports, establishing Somaliland as the leading precious metals hub in the Horn of Africa.
