Strategic Rationale: Why This Partnership Is Necessary

Symbiotic Model
🏛️

Why the Government Needs the Operator

  • USD 30M Private Capital Investment: NorthRock fully funds the entire facility and purchasing liquidity with zero sovereign debt or drawing on national reserves.
  • USD 25M Liquidity Float Captures Artisanal Supply: Raw gold leaves the country because miners require immediate cash. The float provides spot cash settlement, shifting volume into the formal economy.
  • Metallurgical Know-How & DMCC Off-Take: Supplies high-recovery 800L chemical circuits, 400T stamped minting, ICP-OES assay certification, and guaranteed off-take contracts into Dubai bullion markets.
🤝

Why the Operator Needs the Government

  • 25-Year Operating Exclusivity: Deploying USD 30M requires long-term legal security and protection from arbitrary regulatory changes through an executive Presidential Decree.
  • Mandatory Domestic Refining Policy: Requires all gold extracted in Somaliland to be refined in-country before export, securing continuous intake for the 1 MT/week plant.
  • Cooperative Formalization & Security Escorts: The Ministry organizes mining communities into legal supply channels and provides state security escorts between depots and the airport.
