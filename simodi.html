CLOSED-LOOP ARCHITECTURE (LAW NO. 79/2018)

Environmental Safety & Containment Systems

Regulatory StandardMoECC Approved • Zero-Discharge

The proposed Simodi Gold Refinery Facility will operate under a closed-loop environmental architecture designed to secure expedited environmental impact certification under Law No. 79/2018. The compound's industrial containment systems isolate metallurgical processing from the surrounding environment to eliminate heavy metal and acidic contamination.

01 / OFF-GAS SCRUBBINGNOx & SO₂ Neutralization

Atmospheric Emission Control

Multi-stage wet scrubbers continuously capture and neutralize corrosive NOx off-gasses generated during the aqua regia digestion phase.

02 / VAPOR CONDENSATIONASM Smelting Safety

Mercury Abatement System

To process artisanal doré safely, a closed retort system utilizing chilled vapor traps and carbon filters isolates and condenses volatilized mercury, eliminating atmospheric release during the initial induction melting phase.

03 / CLOSED WATER CIRCUITFilter-Press Cake

Zero-Liquid-Discharge (ZLD) Effluent Treatment

The integrated ETP plant chemically neutralizes all acidic process water. Heavy metals are precipitated into stable filter cakes via high-pressure filter presses, ensuring zero hazardous liquid is discharged into municipal systems.

04 / STRUCTURAL ISOLATION1,600 m² Compound

Industrial Containment

The 1,600 Sqm facility structural conversion includes dedicated acid bunding and isolated drainage networks to prevent accidental chemical leaching.
