SECTION 01

WHO WE ARE

TerraQuest Investments FZCO exists to power the next generation of investments across Africa. Our mandate is to transform productive regional sectors—spanning agriculture, trade, manufacturing, and financial infrastructure—into bankable, capital-protected assets ready for institutional scale.

Africa does not lack liquidity; it lacks the de-risked, investment-grade structures required to deploy it. We serve institutional capital at both ends of this divide. For African pension funds, SACCOs, and commercial banks stewarding over $1.5 Trillion in domestic assets, we structure secure, capital-protected assets that allow domestic capital to lead. For GCC family offices, DFIs, and global capital partners, we provide validated market entry—crowding in international liquidity behind domestic first-loss buffers and sovereign credibility to achieve protected, risk-adjusted returns.

In Dubai, our Capital & Structuring Desk—located at Business Bay—leads balance sheet structuring, instrument design, and international liquidity aggregation across the GCC and global markets. In Nairobi, our Regional Execution Desk is anchored by operators with over 25 years of direct leadership across the entire agricultural value chain. Our people have built, owned, and run primary assets at every stage—from commercial seed breeding, rangeland management, and irrigated farming to cold-chain logistics, agro-industrial processing plants, and cross-border trade routes. This hands-on operational heritage connects institutional balance sheets directly to ground execution, ensuring capital is actively managed and safeguarded by people who know how these industries operate from the inside out.

DUBAI DESK GCC Market Coverage with a deep focus on catalytic capital raising strategies, mobilizing institutional balance sheets, sovereign liquidity, and bespoke structuring across global financial centers.
NAIROBI DESK Cross-functional teams executing specialised capital strategies across the entire agricultural value chain, with dedicated focus on Sub-Saharan African markets.

Selected Case Study • Trade Credit

01

Tsavo Global Trade Credit Facility

Status: Ongoing $40M Global Program
Platform Owner TerraQuest Investments FZCO
Counterparty Tsavo (Ug) Trading Ltd
Partners Institutional Off-Takers / Commercial Banks
Country East & Southern Africa
The Context & Solution

Moving food crops across East and Southern Africa is broken by late payments, lack of seasonal liquidity, and constant default risk. TerraQuest created and owns Tsavo Global Trade Credit as a standing trade finance engine. It funds the purchase, storage, and cross-border delivery of core grains (maize, pulses, oilseeds) and export crops (cocoa, coffee).

How It Works

Repo Model: Built around a Master Repurchase Agreement. Tsavo buys grain from farmers and transfers clear warehouse receipts to the facility, agreeing to buy them back at a set forward price once sold to off-takers.

Physical Security: Funds release only against verified inventory held by independent warehouse collateral managers, backed by escrow accounts and comprehensive transit insurance.

Selected Case Study • Food Reserve

02

Tsavo Resilience Food Fund I

Status: Ongoing $400M Target Fund Program
Client Food Reserve Agency of Zambia (FRA Zambia)
Partners Institutional Pension Funds / Grain Processors
Project Type Strategic Food Reserve & Price Stabilization
Country Zambia / Kenya
The Context & Solution

Severe swings in grain prices regularly hurt commercial food processors, squeeze animal feed mills, and threaten regional food security. Working directly with the Food Reserve Agency of Zambia (FRA Zambia), TerraQuest structured this fund to act as an economic shock absorber across Southern and Eastern Africa.

How It Works

Cross-Border Buying: The fund purchases physical grain during harvest surplus in producing regions like Zambia, locking in baseline reserves and releasing stock directly to industrial millers in deficit markets like Kenya.

First-Loss Protection: Sovereign and concessionary capital absorbs initial downside risk, shielding institutional investors from price volatility.

03

TsavoINFRA

Status: Ongoing $100M Infrastructure Program
Client Tsavo (Ug) Trading Ltd
Partners NSSF Uganda / DFI Syndicates
Project Type Post-Harvest Infrastructure & Debt Issuance
Country Sub-Saharan Africa (Anchor: Uganda)
The Context & Solution

East Africa loses up to a third of its harvest simply because drying and storage facilities do not exist. To solve this, TerraQuest structured TsavoINFRA as a multi-year infrastructure program to fund the physical assets required across primary farming belts.

How It Works

Local Capital Anchors First: NSSF Uganda backed the program by taking the subordinated Class A notes. This clear vote of local confidence gave international lenders the comfort to join.

Tangible Assets: Capital flows straight into commercial grain silos, drying towers, sorting facilities, and cold storage hubs.

Selected Case Study • Manufacturing

04

MODA Industrial Processing

Status: Closed – Deployed $50M Program
Client Pick-A-Lot Ltd
Partners Andritz AG (Denmark)
Project Type Agro-Industrial Manufacturing Program
Country Sub-Saharan Africa (Anchor: Kenya)
The Context & Solution

TerraQuest structured and executed the full buyout of a premier Kenyan commercial herb exporter to facilitate a clean exit for its original owner-founder. The business generated $8.9M in baseline annual revenue shipping fresh basil, mint, and coriander to European retailers, but was constrained by succession dynamics and cosmetic grading waste. TerraQuest stepped in as acquiring principal to institutionalise governance and unlock dormant processing capacity.

How It Works

Vendor Financing: Structured a 5-year equipment credit package directly with Danish manufacturer Andritz, including a 6-month grace period.

Captive Energy: Built a closed-loop system where waste maize cobs from the feed plant feed an on-site biomass plant, powering the factory at a fraction of grid rates.

Selected Case Study • Livestock Value Chain

05

Project Ndegwa

Status: Ongoing $100M Capital Raise
Client Kenya Family Office
Partners GCC Family Office
Project Type Commercial Livestock Production & Export
Country Tanzania – Kenya – UAE
The Context & Solution

To build an export pipeline of premium beef, goat, sheep, and camel into the Gulf, TerraQuest structured Project Ndegwa to connect upstream livestock rangelands in Tanzania directly with finishing yards in Kenya and institutional distribution channels in Dubai.

How It Works

Corporate Holding Structure: Used an Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM) holding company to own the upstream operating assets in Kenya and Tanzania alongside a dedicated trading desk in the UAE.

Structured Cash Waterfall: All sales from cattle, goat, sheep, camel, feed crops, and fodder clear through a strict account hierarchy ensuring net profits are split 50/50.

Operational Mechanics: Livestock graze on volcanic rangelands in Tanzania supported by 2,500 acres of irrigated fodder, then finish in Kenya for export to the UAE under zero-tariff trade agreements.
