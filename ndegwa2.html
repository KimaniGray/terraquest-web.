Executive Summary

Project Ndegwa translates the commercial rangeland operations and livestock assets of PineCrest Holdings into a clear, Shariah-compliant investment plan for Golden Nile Trading LLC. Capitalizing on zero-tariff trade rules under the Kenya-UAE Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA), the joint venture establishes an integrated beef export and agribusiness enterprise supplying GCC import markets and regional East African wholesale channels.

The investment program establishes an initial baseline capital deployment of USD 63,600,000 across Phase A, with a secondary value chain expansion commitment of USD 36,400,000 across Phase B, bringing the total potential investment program to USD 100,000,000.

Corporate Structure & Entity Breakdown

  • 1. UAE InvestCo (Holding Level): Incorporated in the Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM) or a Dubai Integrated Economic Zones (DIEZ) special economic zone under English Common Law, UAE InvestCo serves as the neutral holding entity. The company is capitalized with nominal ordinary share capital establishing absolute, non-dilutive 50/50 voting parity between Investor and Investee on Day 1.
  • 2. Kenya OpCo (Upstream Operating Subsidiary): Incorporated in Kenya as a wholly owned subsidiary of UAE InvestCo. Kenya OpCo holds physical industrial assets capitalized directly on its balance sheet, operates standing feedlots and irrigated fodder land under registered 25-year commercial leases, and manages the Bovine IVF laboratory. 100% of slaughtering, seam-boning, portion-cutting, and packaging takes place at origin in Kenya.
  • 3. UAE OpCo (Downstream Commercial Trading): Incorporated in the UAE as an asset-light trading subsidiary of UAE InvestCo. UAE OpCo holds binding GCC off-take sales agreements, acts as importer of record clearing shipments under CEPA zero-tariff preferences, manages leased third-party (3PL) cold storage in KEZAD and JAFZA, and invoices institutional accounts directly in AED and SAR.
  • 4. Intercompany Commercial Bridge: Kenya OpCo and UAE OpCo operate under an arm's-length Master Supply & Off-Take Agreement. Commercial transfers execute under a cost-plus formula benchmarked by independent tax advisors to comply with Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) and UAE Federal Tax Authority (FTA) regulations.
2.

The Operating Model

Operational execution is split between upstream protein production in Kenya and downstream commercial distribution across the GCC, governed by the Master Supply & Off-Take Agreement:

  • Upstream Production (Kenya OpCo): Manages standing finishing yards for 18,000 cattle, an on-site 15 MT/hr automated feed mill, mechanical soybean extruders, irrigated lucerne dehydration, 13,000 MT of corrugated steel grain storage, an assisted reproductive Bovine IVF cleanroom laboratory, and an export-grade Halal abattoir. Processing at source ensures that only packaged, high-yield primal and retail portions enter international transit.
  • Downstream Commercial Distribution (UAE OpCo): Functions without fixed plant liabilities, contracting accredited 3PL temperature-controlled warehousing, managing port and airport customs clearances under CEPA 0% tariffs, and distributing directly to institutional hospitality, retail, and wholesale buyers across the GCC.
  • 45-Day Transition Protocol: Commencing on the initial Funding Date, the Parties execute a 45-day operational migration, completing subsidiary incorporations, executive leadership recruitment, and novation of legacy commercial contracts, leading to the Cut-Off Date for full operational handover to Kenya OpCo.

5.

Treasury, Distribution & Settlement

The joint venture operates a dual treasury collection structure. Export receivables are collected in AED and USD by UAE OpCo, while regional African trade receipts are collected in KES and USD by Kenya OpCo. All gross commercial trade receipts across both operating subsidiaries are swept and settled through the Four-Tier Distribution Waterfall before any quarterly equity dividends are distributed under Tier 4.

Four-Tier Distribution Waterfall
Tier Priority Category Settlement Rule & Formula Currency Flow
TIER 1 Direct Operating COGS Gross trade receipts immediately clear logistics, processing utilities, animal feed, consumables, and operational retainers. AED / SAR / USD
TIER 2 Priority Capital Recovery Systematic amortization of deployed Istisna'a and Murabaha capital plus agreed profit rate; swept without diluting equity parity. Swept to InvestCo
TIER 3 Operating Reserves Mandatory replenishment of rolling 60-day feeder livestock purchase float, grain buffer, and critical packaging consumables. Retained Cash
TIER 4 Equal Profit Sharing Residual net operational profits distributed equally as ordinary dividends (50% Investee / 50% Investor). Quarterly Dividend

This cash flow mechanism protects enterprise liquidity prior to equity profit distributions. By prioritizing operating costs (Tier 1) and continuous procurement reserves (Tier 3), the venture maintains continuous cattle finishing and plant operations. Tier 2 amortization systematically repays capital without altering the foundational 50/50 ordinary voting parity governing net operational profits (Tier 4).
