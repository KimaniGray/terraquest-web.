1.

Executive Summary

Project Ndegwa translates the commercial vision of PineCrest Holdings into a clear, Shariah-compliant investment plan for Golden Nile Trading LLC. By capitalizing on zero-tariff trade under the Kenya-UAE Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA), the joint venture establishes a vertically integrated protein, feed, and export enterprise targeting the GCC and East Africa.

The investment rolls out across a unified two-phase program establishing a USD 100,000,000 total baseline commitment (split symmetrically at USD 50 million per phase). It includes optional 30% Accordion Tranches, providing expansion headroom up to an aggregate authorized envelope of USD 130,000,000.

2.

The Operating Model

Operational execution is cleanly divided between two dedicated entities operating under an arm's-length Master Supply & Off-Take Agreement utilizing an economic cost-plus formula compliant with KRA and UAE Corporate Tax rules:

  • Kenya OpCo (Upstream Production): Holds all operational assets, facilities, leases, and biological stock in Kenya. It operates under 25-year commercial registered leases over feedlot yards and fodder acreage. It directly owns the abattoir, cold chain, 15 MT/hr feed mill, bulk steel silos, Bovine IVF laboratory, and tannery capitalized directly on its balance sheet. It manages the 18,000-head feedlot finishing yards and procures livestock under Shariah-compliant contracts. 100% of seam-boning, portioning, retail skin-packaging, and cooked charcuterie is executed at origin in Kenya to maximize domestic labor cost advantages and eliminate air-freighting waste bones to the Gulf.
  • UAE OpCo (Downstream Logistics): Operates as an asset-light commercial sales, trading, and distribution subsidiary in the UAE. It signs binding GCC off-take sales contracts, clears import customs under CEPA zero-tariff preferences, manages leased third-party (3PL) bonded cold-storage staging in KEZAD/JAFZA, coordinates last-mile refrigerated fulfillment, and invoices institutional supermarket and foodservice accounts in local currencies (AED and SAR).

Supply Chain Execution Roadmap

Operational execution activates the Investee's foundational assets into a high-throughput protein facility across a strict flow. The process is governed by a 45-Day Transition Protocol, triggering a unified Cut-Off Date for total commercial takeover by Kenya OpCo.

3.

Corporate Governance & Control

UAE InvestCo is established in the Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM), providing direct application of English Common Law and institutional Islamic finance recognition. The joint venture is governed as a Shariah-compliant Musharaka partnership, capitalized with a nominal paid-up ordinary share capital maintaining absolute, non-dilutive 50/50 ordinary voting parity between Investor and Investee.

Board & Committee Structure

Corporate authority sits with a lean, highly aligned 5-member Board of Directors (two Investee appointees, two Investor appointees, and one mutually agreed Independent Agribusiness Director) operating without third-party kingmaker bottlenecks. Strategic Reserved Matters require an affirmative 4/5 supermajority.

Executive Committee (ExCo)

Composition: One (1) Investor Rep + One (1) Investee Rep. Executive leadership participates without voting rights.

Decision Rule: Bilateral sponsor consensus.

Scope: Routine operational oversight across all facilities, cattle intake pricing formulas, and line-item variances within Account 3 up to ±15%.

Audit, Risk & Transfer Pricing (AR-TP)

Composition: Investee Director (Chair), Investor Director, Group COO, Group CFO, and statutory auditor.

Scope: Financial audit oversight, UAE Corporate Tax and KRA transfer pricing benchmark verification, and DVS/MoCCaE export biosecurity compliance.

The capital framework strictly separates enterprise equity from asset-backed Islamic funding. Deployed project capital across Phase 1 and Phase 2 is non-recourse to the Investee's foundational breeding herds and ancestral rangeland titles. Under no circumstances does capital recovery or Accordion drawdowns dilute the equal 50/50 ordinary voting parity established on Day 1.

Phase 1 front-loads feed industrialization to secure live finishing costs at substantial discounts to the open market. It establishes the baseline production footprint, including the 15 MT/hr feed mill, bulk steel silos, Bovine IVF laboratory, and the Halal export abattoir.

5.

Treasury, Distribution & Summary

The joint venture operates a dual cash collection system. Export receivables are collected in AED/USD by UAE OpCo, while domestic and regional African receivables are collected in KES/USD by Kenya OpCo. All gross commercial receipts across both entities are strictly swept and settled through the unified Four-Tier Distribution Waterfall before any equity profit distributions are paid under Tier 4.

Four-Tier Distribution Waterfall
Tier Priority Category Settlement Rule & Formula Currency Flow
TIER 1 Direct Operating COGS Gross customer trade proceeds clear freight, processing, feed, consumables, and priority retainers. AED / SAR / USD
TIER 2 Priority Capital Recovery Amortization of deployed Istisna'a/Murabaha capital plus agreed return, swept without dilution. Swept to InvestCo
TIER 3 Operating Reserves Mandatory replenishment of rolling 60-day feeder cattle intake and grain/consumable stocks. Retained Cash
TIER 4 Equal Profit Sharing Residual net operational profits distributed equally (50% Investee / 50% Investor). Quarterly Dividend

Stage 07

Appointment of C-Suite Executive Leadership & Operational Implementation

Board appoints the Group CEO, Group COO, and Group CFO to assume executive control, manage the unified Cut-Off Date, and execute physical works.
