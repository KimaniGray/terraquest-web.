1.

Executive Summary & Asset Parameters

Addendum 01 introduces a strategic regional expansion option for Project Ndegwa: the acquisition and operational integration of Mountain Side Farms, a rare 9,500-acre titled estate situated in West Kilimanjaro, Tanzania. Located within close proximity to Project Ndegwa's primary rangelands in Narok, Kenya, Mountain Side Farms provides immediate access to the vast Tanzanian national cattle herd (34M+ head), large-scale irrigated fodder multiplication, and high-margin eco-tourism hospitality.

The estate combines immediate operational cash flows (avocado exports and grain farming) with transformational strategic synergies for Project Ndegwa, buffering fodder security for the Narok and Kitengela feedlots while establishing a high-yield livestock backgrounding corridor in Northern Tanzania.

2.

Regional Geography & Supply Synergies

Mountain Side Farms occupies a pivotal position in West Kilimanjaro, directly across the southern border from Project Ndegwa's primary rangelands in Narok. This geography enables a cross-border agricultural corridor connecting livestock aggregation in Tanzania to processing and export infrastructure in Kenya and the UAE.

  • 1. Tanzania Beef Corridor Access: Tanzania holds Africa's second-largest livestock herd (34M+ cattle). Mountain Side Farms serves as Project Ndegwa's primary backgrounding and quarantine hub, aggregating young bulls from pastoralist networks across Northern Tanzania.
  • 2. Proximity to Narok Hub: Situated near the Kenyan border, feeder cattle backgrounded at West Kilimanjaro cross seamlessly via the Namanga or Holili border posts into Project Ndegwa's Narok feedlots for final intensive finishing.
  • 3. Export Route Optimization: Agricultural produce and processed beef enter international transit via Kenya's logistics network (Jomo Kenyatta International Airport and Mombasa Port), benefiting from zero-tariff access into the UAE under the Kenya-UAE CEPA.
3.

Four Commercial Enterprise Pillars

Mountain Side Farms diversifies Project Ndegwa's operating base across four high-margin commercial pillars:

1. Livestock Aggregation

Establishes a 10,000-head backgrounding operation. Aggregates native Tanzanian zebu breeds, applying Boran embryo genetic upgrades from Project Ndegwa's IVF cleanroom laboratory to improve carcase yields.

2. Irrigated Fodder Buffer

Deploys center-pivot irrigation across 2,500 acres for Rhodes grass, lucerne, and silage production. Provides dedicated feed security to insulate Project Ndegwa's Narok and Kitengela finishing yards from drought.

3. Luxury Eco-Tourism Retreat

Develops a 16-to-24 key luxury safari camp on the 900-acre pristine wilderness section (~7 km from Mount Kilimanjaro's Londorossi Gate), capturing premium mountaineering and conservation tourism revenue.

4. Commercial Crop Exports

Optimizes existing Hass avocado orchards and commercial wheat rotations, leveraging Project Ndegwa's UAE trading subsidiary (UAE OpCo) to distribute premium produce directly to GCC retail channels.

4.

Project Ndegwa Structuring Strategies

To acquire and operationalise Mountain Side Farms within Project Ndegwa, two distinct transaction structures are presented for review by the Investment Committee and Board of Directors:

Strategy 01: Joint Equity Acquisition (SPV Buyout)

UAE InvestCo incorporates a wholly owned subsidiary (Tanzania SPV) to acquire 100% of the titles and assets of Mountain Side Farms for USD 28,000,000.

  • Funding Mechanism: Funded via Investor asset-backed capital commitment appended to Account 2.
  • Capital Amortization: Acquisition capital is amortized under Tier 2 of Project Ndegwa's Revenue Waterfall.
  • Governance: Fully consolidated under Project Ndegwa's non-dilutive 50/50 ordinary equity parity.

Strategy 02: Investee Acquisition & Joint Operationalisation

The Investee acquires the land title unencumbered on its balance sheet (held as an Excluded Asset), ring-fencing land ownership while executing long-term commercial operational leases with Project Ndegwa.

  • 25-Year Commercial Lease: Investee grants a 25-year operational lease over grazing and fodder acreage to Kenya OpCo / Tanzania OpCo at Cost-Plus rates.
  • Hospitality Joint Venture: A dedicated 50/50 operating vehicle (Hospitality OpCo) is established to build and operate the 900-acre luxury eco-lodge.
  • Asset Protection: Land ownership remains unencumbered, shielding core real estate from enterprise liabilities.
5.

Treasury & Waterfall Integration

All operational revenues generated by Mountain Side Farms (livestock transfers, fodder sales, avocado export receipts, and hospitality income) are fully integrated into Project Ndegwa's central treasury administration and swept through the established Four-Tier Distribution Waterfall:

Integration with Project Ndegwa Four-Tier Waterfall
Tier Category Expansion Cash Flow Application
TIER 1 Direct OPEX & COGS Clears farm labor, irrigation power, feed mill inputs, veterinary supplies, and lease charges.
TIER 2 Priority Capital Amortization Systematically repays expansion CAPEX (irrigation pivots, eco-lodge buildout, acquisition debt).
TIER 3 Operating Reserves Replenishes rolling 60-day livestock purchase float and fodder reserve buffer.
TIER 4 50/50 Profit Share Residual operational net profits distributed equally as ordinary dividends (50% Investor / 50% Investee).

This treasury structure guarantees that expansion revenues reinforce enterprise liquidity, ensure capital recovery, and protect equal dividend distributions under Project Ndegwa's governance framework.
