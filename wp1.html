THE PRODUCT VISION

Sub-Saharan Africa faces an annual trade finance deficit exceeding USD 120 billion (~KES 5.54 trillion) driven by structural dollar liquidity shortages and commercial bank retrenchment. The SIB–Terraquest partnership converts high-yielding, senior-secured trade finance transactions into institutional-grade, capital-preserved asset allocations for retirement funds.

Statutory Definition: Regulation 2 of Retirement Benefits (Forms and Fees) Regulations, 2000
"Approved Issuer" =

[A] An insurer registered under Insurance Act (Cap. 487)
                                 OR 
[B] Any other issuer approved in writing under Capital Markets Act (Cap. 485A) or written law
Statutory Parity

Insurance dominance of this asset clas refects historical underwritting habit rather than statutory excusivity. The Reteriment Benefit Act provides specifically for capital market liencees to develop a guranteed fund.

Non-Insurance Scope

SIB underwrites capital preservation and fixed returns without actuarial risk, operating under Section 24 of Cap. 485A.

100% Portfolio Ceiling

Under Table G (Category 4), retirement schemes may allocate up to 100% of fund assets into Approved Issuer guaranteed funds.

2.2 Corporate Governance Structure

  • Housing Vehicle: TRIFIC SEZ SPV serves as passive capital conduit with zero staff or operational liabilities.
  • Equity Parity: 50/50 shareholding between SIB and Terraquest for corporate governance control.
  • Board Composition: 5 seats total — 2 SIB directors, 2 Terraquest directors, and 1 Independent Chair.
  • Negative Veto: SIB holds an unyielding negative veto at Investment Committee level over every pipeline asset.

2.3 Accounting Treatment & FX Mechanics

  • IAS 28 / IFRS 11 Equity Method: JV accounted off-balance sheet as net investment line item, shielding SIB's balance sheet.
  • Regulatory Capital Shield: Preserves CMA capital adequacy and liquid ratio compliance without recognizing USD 400M debt.
  • Currency Mechanics: KES subscriptions converted to USD at spot on receipt, securing hard-currency principal lock.
  • Yield Spread Mechanics: Underlying trade book earns 9.0%–12.0% gross p.a. against a 6.0% net guaranteed floor.

Structural Entity Flow & Credit Hierarchy

The corporate structure strictly separates institutional investors, the contracting vehicle, the legal asset holder, and the final borrowers. This establishes a clean, legally enforceable credit hierarchy that ensures bankruptcy remoteness at every tier.

3.1 Credit Hierarchy & STRUCTURING

1. Pension Schemes (The Investors):Contractual senior debt holders under the Master Guaranteed Fund Agreement, with direct rights to the 6.0% USD guaranteed floor without taking equity or operating risk in the SPV.
2. TRIFIC SEZ SPV (Capital Housing):Special Economic Zone entity incorporated inside Two Rivers SEZ. Executes the guarantee contracts, houses capital flows, and interfaces with the RBA-licensed Custodian under preferential tax treaties.
3. TRFF Sub-SPVs (Bankruptcy-Remote Vehicles):Dedicated Trade Receivables Finance Facilities (LLCs) holding specific loan portfolios and pledge instruments. Each vehicle is 100% owned by an RBA-licensed Independent Corporate Trustee.
4. Corporate Obligors (Borrowers):Sovereign bodies (e.g., Food Reserve Agency of Zambia), state utilities, and blue-chip trading conglomerates issuing short-term, self-liquidating trade paper with clear legal recourse.

Asset Deployment FRAMEWORK

4.1 Permitted Trade Universe

The vehicle deploys capital exclusively into self-liquidating, short-term Structured Trade Products. Unsecured debt and speculative trading are strictly prohibited:

  • Sovereign Agencies: Central Banks, Food Reserve Agencies (FRA Zambia repo), and state-owned energy utilities.
  • Multinational Corporates: Tier-1 FMCG, telecommunications, and industrial off-takers with verified credit lines.
  • Commodity Aggregators: Top-tier international trading houses backed by binding and verified offtake contracts.
  • Financial Institutions: Avalized promissory notes and confirmed irrevocable documentary letters of credit.

4.2 Single Deployment Architecture

High-conviction institutional deployment architecture designed for ring-fenced credit execution:

  • Tranche Concentration: Statutory authority to deploy up to 100% of an individual tranche into an isolated sovereign facility.
  • Isolation Remoteness: Tranches are partitioned in discrete TRFF LLCs, eliminating cross-cohort or cross-facility contagion.
  • Tenor Limits: Strict 365-day maximum tenor limits, maintaining 90–180 day weighted average liquidity turnover.
5.0

Structured De-Risking & Protection Hierarchy

Protection is engineered through institutional Secured Trade Finance Structuring rather than relying on third-party insurance as its primary safeguard:

Layer 1 (Collateral)  ➔  Layer 2 (Escrow/IPU)  ➔  Layer 3 (Reserves)  ➔  Layer 4 (SIB Backstop)
Layer 1 • Physical Security ~150% Cover
Senior Secured Trade Collateral & Title Retention

Perfected first-priority fixed charge over underlying traded commodities, electronic warehouse receipts, or confirmed bills of lading with mandatory over-collateralization haircuts.

Layer 2 • Cash Controls Tripartite
Irrevocable Payment Undertakings & Escrow

Commercial buyer and sovereign payments completely bypass borrower operating accounts, flowing directly into tripartite cash collection escrow accounts controlled by the Custodian.

Layer 3 • Sovereign Liquidity 1.0% - 1.5%
Sovereign Repositories & Programme Cash Reserves

Binding repurchase undertakings by sovereign agencies/ministries of finance, backed by a dedicated segregated cash liquidity reserve maintained at the Programme level.

Layer 4 • Institutional Floor Contractual
SIB Approved Issuer Corporate Guarantee Backstop

SIB's primary contractual undertaking assuring schemes timely return of 100% invested principal and the guaranteed 6.00% net hard-currency floor under Cap. 485A.
