Chapter 02

The Mechanism

We do not take agricultural production risk. The Fund executes a structured Commercial Repurchase Agreement (Repo) on post-harvest, secured commodity stocks.

  • 1. The Fund acquires legal title to Maize at a ~30% discount to market price.
  • 2. Assets are placed in bonded, insured custody.
  • 3. The Food Reserve Agency (FRA) executes a forward contract to repurchase at a fixed premium.
