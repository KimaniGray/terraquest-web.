Chapter 01 — The Core Mechanism

The Repo + Plus Structure

This is not an unsecured loan. We deploy capital through a structured "True Sale" Commodity Repurchase Agreement, moving through three highly controlled stages to guarantee a fixed exit[cite: 6, 7]:

  • 1. Purchase: The SPV acquires absolute legal title to White Non-GMO Maize at a deep ~30% discount to market price, evidenced by statutory Warehouse Receipts (WHRs)[cite: 6, 7].
  • 2. Repurchase: The Food Reserve Agency (FRA) executes a forward contract to repurchase the commodity at a fixed, pre-determined premium within 12 months[cite: 6, 7].
  • 3. Liquidation (The Safety Valve): If the FRA delays, the "Maize or Cash" protocol activates. The Security Trustee automatically shifts the exit strategy to secondary market liquidation, executing pre-arranged offtake agreements (e.g., the 540,000 MT capacity with APAK in Kenya) to extinguish the debt[cite: 7].
