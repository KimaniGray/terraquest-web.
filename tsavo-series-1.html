The Market Context [cite: 182]

Zambia occupies a unique and powerful geopolitical position as the undisputed "grain basket" of Southern Africa. Its strategic importance is magnified by its dual membership in SADC and COMESA, granting the Fund seamless, duty-free market access to over 600 million people. [cite: 184, 185, 186]

The Zambian Surplus

The 2025/2026 season marks a definitive structural shift. Driven by favorable weather patterns and enhanced input subsidies, Zambia has recorded a massive bumper harvest. The FRA holds a confirmed reserve of ~2 Million bags of White Maize. [cite: 191, 192]

The Regional Deficit

While Zambia enters a surplus cycle, the broader region faces severe structural deficits due to persistent drought. This creates a massive, quantifiable "Demand Pull" quantified at over 6 Million Metric tonnes. [cite: 195, 196]
