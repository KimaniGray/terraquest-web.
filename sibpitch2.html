EXECUTION PATHWAYS

1. Asset Management ($300M)

As a CMA-regulated Investment Bank, SIB legally qualifies as an "Approved Issuer" under RBA guidelines. By wrapping the secured TRFF-1 Notes within a new SIB Guaranteed Fund, you can absorb the $300M market scale-up. Offering a tax-sheltered, double-digit USD yield will grow your Asset Management AUM away from legacy insurers.

2. Principal Warehousing & Private Distribution ($80M)

SIB's Fixed Income division can use internal capital to execute a "Bought Deal," warehousing a wholesale block of the private debt to syndicate to your private wealth and corporate networks.

Target Wholesale Block$50M to $80M
Warehousing Period30 to 90 days
Average Arbitrage Spread1.50% to 2.50%

The Mechanics:

  1. Master Registry: The Note & Security Trustee (MTC Trust) maintains a private Noteholder Register.
  2. Wholesale Purchase: SIB takes down a $50M-$80M block at the wholesale yield (~15.00%).
  3. Secondary Sell-Down: SIB syndicates smaller tickets ($1M–$5M) to corporate treasuries and HNWIs at a retail yield (e.g., 12.50%–13.50%).
  4. Clearing the Trade: SIB submits the Transfer Certificate to MTC Trust, deducting the amount from SIB’s block and adding the client as the new beneficial owner.
  5. Cash Settlement: The buyer wires the purchase funds directly to SIB, allowing SIB to instantly capture the arbitrage spread.

3. Mansa-X & Najah ($20M)

Simultaneously, the SPV accommodates direct allocations from your flagship CIS portfolios. Mansa-X and SIB Najah can take a direct wholesale position in the underlying physical commodity trade, capturing the full ~15% USD yield to hedge against KES depreciation and boost overall fund performance.
