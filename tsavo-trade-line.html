05 Flow of Funds

Cycle 1: Discount & Disbursement

1. Instrument Issuance

Buyer issues Drawing Instrument (MT700) to the Trade Agent.

2. Discounting

Trade Agent (Zebu) discounts the instrument and releases funds.

3. Deal Checker & Disbursement

Advisor reviews documents and controls disbursements against pre-agreed use of funds.

4. Seller Payment

Seller receives working capital strictly tied to value creation.

Cycle 2: Repayment & Recycling

1. Value Realization

Buyer receives goods, processes, and generates revenue.

2. Deal Checker Review

TerraQuest reviews all payment documents for compliance, presenting risk-cleared files to the Trade Agent.

3. Maturity Settlement

Buyer settles the outstanding balance (O/S) to the Trade Agent.

4. Credit Line Recycled

The capital pool is replenished and reinstated for the next cycle.

06 Operational Mechanics

A.

Pre-Discounting & Structuring

01

Cash Flow Mapping: Trade Advisor maps the supplier's funding gaps and structures precise intervention points.

02

Contract Validation: Buyer confirms the supply contract and issues an irrevocable payment undertaking (MT700).

03

Use of Funds Locked: Capital deployment is mapped strictly to raw materials, logistics, or utility payments.

B.

Post-Discounting

04

Tsavo executes managed disbursements to the targeted value-creation nodes; receivable assigned to SPV.

05

Deal Checker Review: A dedicated office verifies all settlement documents for compliance, presenting cleared files to the Trade Agent.

06

At maturity, Buyer settles full face value to Tsavo trust account; funds distributed to capital providers.
