06 Operational Mechanics

A.

Pre-Discounting

01

Exporter delivers goods; Importer validates invoice and issues irrevocable payment undertaking.

02

Tsavo Facility performs KYC/AML on Buyer and verifies transaction against origin shipping docs.

03

Insurance binder activated for specific transaction tranche, providing 90% principal protection.

B.

Post-Discounting

04

Tsavo wires discounted proceeds to Exporter; ownership of receivable legally assigned to SPV.

05

Digital ledger tracks asset through maturity; automated reminders sent to Buyer's treasury team.

06

At maturity, Buyer settles 100% face value to Tsavo trust account; funds distributed to investors.
