Product Overview

A non-recourse receivable purchase facility co-engineered to bridge critical working capital gaps in emerging market value chains.

  • Regional Focus: Intended for the East, Central and Southern Africa-Sub Saharan Facility, focusing on energy, mining, and food & agriculture sectors.
  • Commercial Balance: Transforms illiquid cross-border transactions into immediate, institutional-grade capital for the Seller, while granting the Buyer working capital runway.
  • Alternative Liquidity: Deepens markets through private institutional liquidity, allowing credible suppliers to produce and supply at scale outside traditional retail banking constraints.

Key Product Features

Flexible Usance

Provides off-balance sheet deferred payment terms, granting international or local buyers the crucial time needed to process, distribute, and generate revenue before settlement.

Accelerated Liquidity

Deploys up to 89% of the invoice value to the value chain immediately upon destination acceptance, eliminating the standard wait periods associated with deferred credit.

Non-Recourse Structure

Once the trade is discounted by institutional partners, the Seller holds no repayment liability regarding bank default risk, securely converting a future receivable into present capital.

Revolving Capacity

Engineered to be scalable, the facility supports continuous, multi-shipment supply chains, effectively increasing annual trade throughput without tying up corporate equity.

The Value Proposition

For the Seller (Applicant)

  • Unlock Capital Velocity: Secure immediate cash flow upon arrival, funding subsequent export cycles without delay.
  • Access Institutional Capital: Tap into specialized liquidity pools outside retail banking to scale export capacity.
  • Non-Recourse Liquidity: Convert guaranteed receivables into cash without assuming bank default risk.
  • Asset Monetization: Optimize your balance sheet without the burden of traditional bank debt.

For the Buyer

  • Supply Chain Continuity: Guarantee your procurement pipeline by enabling your suppliers to produce at full capacity.
  • Revenue-First Settlement: Generate revenue from commodities before your bank is required to settle the payment.
  • Risk-Free Procurement: Financial obligations mature only after goods arrive and pass physical inspection.
  • Strategic Support: Leverage your strong balance sheet to offer a bank instrument (MT700) that secures supply.

Flow of Funds Architecture (Closed-Loop)

1

Instrument Issuance

Buyer issues MT700 from a credible bank to Trade Agent (Zebu).

2

Discounting

Upon verification, Zebu discounts the undertaking into Zebu Trade Account.

CONTINUOUS DE-RISKING: TerraQuest Trade Accounts Office (TAO) controls documents & prepares pre-agreed disbursements.
3

Managed Disbursement

Funds released strictly against UOF directly to ecosystem participants.

4

Seller Settlement

Remaining net margin paid to Seller to fulfill working capital surplus.

5

Capital Recycling

At maturity, Buyer's bank settles MT700, replenishing the pool for next cycle.

Operational Mechanics

Defined by rigorous risk mitigation and strategic cash flow control, moving beyond simple lending into active trade management.

Pre-Discounting (Foundation)

  • Deep Due Diligence: Comprehensive vetting of the Seller's production history and the Buyer's balance sheet strength.
  • Strategic Structuring: Engineering the Term Sheet and MT700 to sync perfectly, mandating origin inspection as a performance benchmark.
  • Source Linkage: Linking the facility directly to the underlying trade source to meet all compliance and KYC standards.

Post-Discounting (Compliance)

  • Leakage Prevention: Active control of cash flow through the TerraQuest TAO via managed disbursements.
  • Operational Directing: Ensuring capital velocity fuels the supply chain (producers, logistics) rather than unrelated overhead.
  • Compliance Monitoring: Ongoing oversight until final settlement to capture trade data for revolving assessments.

Product Lifecycle

1. Origination

TerraQuest & Zebu conduct joint DD. Term Sheet is signed.

2. Issuance

Buyer's bank issues the MT700 to Standard Chartered London.

3. Production

Seller aggregates commodities, secures origin inspection & ships.

4. Arrival

Goods arrive. Buyer inspects, signs Acceptance, triggering SWIFT.

5. Monetization

Zebu discounts the undertaking. Funds wired via Managed Disbursement.

6. Settlement

Buyer generates revenue. At maturity, bank settles, reinstating facility.
