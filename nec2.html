Lender Diagnostic Instrument

Table 2: Proposed Lender Questionnaire

Focus areas 1 through 4: Portfolio baselines, product structuring, underwriting governance, and fund disbursement integrity.

1. Portfolio Baseline, Agri-Lending Appetite and Potential fund base.

Metric: Agricultural Portfolio Growth & Risk Appetite Index (APGRA)
• What’s your total loan book?
• What percentage of your total gross loan book is allocated to agriculture?
• Do you support the Agro-value chain and what products do you support customers with?
• Which value chains and borrower segments (smallholder, outgrower schemes, commercial) are prioritized?
• What are your current vs. target NPL ratios in agriculture compared to other loan books?
• Would verified cohort repayment baselines lower entry barriers for new value chains?
• Does historical transaction consistency justify expanding total Agri-portfolio limits?
• What is the target loan book expansion (%)?
• Do you have any priority budgets to design and/or implement data lending tools?
• Do you have a base of grant support or technical assistance facilities that allow the business to develop Agri tools for the customer base?

2. Product Design & Credit Structuring

Metric: Agronomy-Linked Credit Structuring Potential (ACSP)
• Do you have specific facilities designed for Agri-business? Or are you in the process of developing this? If not, are Agri-business products of interest in the short/medium or long term? What does your credit product development lifecycle look like?
• Of the products you have, what's their repayment vs security design- bullet repayment, seasonal amortization, input loans, trade finance?
• Are repayment schedules dynamically mapped to crop phenology and harvest calendars, or fixed calendar tenors?
• What data tools or agronomic inputs are currently used during product modeling?
• Can satellite-derived crop cycle timelines automate loan tenors and moratorium periods?
• Would predictive yield modeling enable structured input financing against expected output?

3. Underwriting, Risk Rating & Collateral

Metric: Supply Chain Working Capital & Credit Scaling Potential (SWCCSP)
• What is your current credit scoring process?
• What percentage of loans require traditional physical collateral vs. cash-flow underwriting?
• How do you assess creditworthiness for unbanked smallholders lacking credit bureau history?
• How do you evaluate counterparty risk when lending to farmer groups or aggregator-backed schemes?
• Can verified digital procurement and payout histories replace traditional land/asset collateral for unsecured risk rating?
• Can historical credit default benchmarks be integrated directly into your scorecard to pre-approve farmers?

4. Disbursement Governance (Retail & Wholesale/Non-Bank)

Metric: Closed-Loop Payment Integration Potential (CPIP) Note: Reduction in fund diversion risk; preference for direct-to-vendor payment rails.
• How are funds disbursed (mobile money, bank transfer, direct voucher to input agro-dealers, or bulk line to aggregators)?
• For non-retail lenders (wholesale to cooperatives/agribusinesses), how do you monitor onward utilization and prevent input diversion?
• What is the operational cost and leakage rate during disbursement?
• Can closed-loop payment rails verify genuine input purchases and direct vendor disbursements?
• Can remote boundary and planting verification act as a prerequisite condition precedent (CP) before releasing secondary tranches?

Lender Diagnostic Instrument (Cont.)

Table 2: Proposed Lender Questionnaire (Continued)

Focus areas 5 through 9: In-season intelligence, off-taker settlement, default verification, technical integration, and regulatory boundaries.

5. In-Season Monitoring & Early Warning

Metric: Commercial Solution Addressable Potential (CSAP)
• How do credit officers monitor crop health and field progress post-disbursement?
• What is the annual budget and frequency for on-site farm inspections?
• How are regional climate shocks (drought, flood, pest outbreaks) detected prior to maturity?
• Can satellite indices (NDVI, soil moisture, rainfall anomalies) eliminate 50%+ of physical farm inspection costs?
• Would automated parametric alerts trigger early loan restructuring before technical default occurs?

6. Harvest Settlement & Off-taker Risk

Metric: Speed-Verified Portfolio Recovery Yield Potential (VPRY)
• How are repayments captured at harvest (voluntary branch/mobile repayment, check-off via off-taker, warehouse receipt)?
• Do you bank? Or receive all payments from customers? How do you detect and mitigate side-selling when lending under contract farming or outgrower schemes?
• What mechanisms track actual crop yields against pre-season expectations?
• Can yield prediction benchmarks detect production-to-delivery discrepancies indicative of side-selling?
• Can automated source-deduction integrations execute split-payments directly upon produce delivery?

7. Collections, and Default Baselines

Metric: Speed Verified Portfolio Recovery Yield potential (VPRY)
• How do you verify whether a default is driven by genuine agronomic/climate distress versus willful strategic default (moral hazard)?
• What are your current recovery benchmarks for written-off Agri-debt?
• Can objective satellite weather and loss verification substantiate distress for restructuring or insurance triggers?
• Can historical smallholder repayment velocity models improve collection sequencing and recovery targeting?

8. Systems Integration, Tech Stack, Commercial and Data Privacy

Metric: API Commercialization & Integration Readiness potential (ACIR)
• What Core Banking System (CBS) and Loan Origination System (LOS) are currently deployed?
• Does your IT infrastructure support real-time API queries, or is batch file processing required?
• What pricing model have you tried previously on any solution? commercially viable? per-API query fee, basis points on disbursed volume?, or enterprise annual license?
• What is the maximum acceptable cost per credit check / underwritten loan (USD/KES)?

9. Regulatory Framework

Metric: Regulatory Compliance & Innovation Readiness Index (RCIRI)
• What are the main regulatory compliance and data protection requirements (e.g., Data Protection Act, CBK/SASRA/Ministry guidelines) governing the integration of third-party digital credit scoring and underwriting tools?
• What customer consent mechanisms and data localization/sovereignty protocols are required when sharing farmer transaction or geolocation data with external tech platforms?
• What regulatory approval frameworks or sandbox engagements are needed before rolling out novel digital lending products or automated credit decisioning models?

Underwriting & Actuarial Diagnostic

Proposed Insurance Business Market Validation Questionnaire

Calibrated for licensed insurers under the Insurance Regulatory Authority (IRA) governing agricultural lines, parametric triggers, and credit life covers.

Under the Insurance Act (Cap 487), IRA classifies insurers into 14 general and 7 long-term lines. This sprint focuses on key licenses driving agricultural risk, micro-insurance, and credit safety nets: Core ACIP Validation Target Licenses (2026 IRA Classification)

IRA Class Count Scope Strategic Relevance
Class 14: Agriculture 32 General Crops, livestock, aquaculture, & parametric risk. Primary Target: Leverages historical satellite & weather data to price yield covers.
Class 13: Microinsurance 5 Micro Low-premium smallholder coverage. High Fit: Uses digital registries and rails for instant distribution & payouts.
Class 35: Group Credit 22 Life Group borrower loan protection against major shocks. High Fit: Uses credit profiles to price bundled credit-life products.
Class 37: Investment 18 Life Long-term contingency structures. Strategic Fit: Pairs multi-year capital with aggregator-level parametric covers.

1. Underwriting Portfolio Baseline & Agri-Appetite

Metric: Agricultural Portfolio Expansion & Risk Appetite Index (APGRA)
• What is your gross written premium (GWP) for agricultural insurance (crop & livestock) over the past 3 years?
• What is your current loss ratio in the agricultural line versus other general insurance classes?
• Which agricultural value chains (e.g., maize, tea, coffee, dairy, horticulture) do you currently underwrite, and which are prioritized for expansion?
• What is the distribution mix between standalone agricultural policies and bundled credit-linked insurance products?

2. Actuarial Modeling, Baseline Data & Basis Risk

Metric: Commercial Solution Addressable Potential (CSAP)
• What historical datasets (meteorological, remote sensing, crop cut experiments) do you use to price crop insurance products?
• How do you measure, manage, and mitigate basis risk in index-based and parametric insurance covers?
• Would access to high-resolution, historical satellite-derived agronomic baselines (10+ years) enable you to underwrite unserved value chains?

3. Parametric Product Structuring & Index Calibration

Metric: Crop-Stage Parametric Cover Potential (CPCP)
• Do you offer multi-peril crop insurance (MPCI), area yield index insurance (AYII), or weather index insurance (WII)?
• How do you define index trigger levels and exit points across distinct phenological stages (germination, vegetative, flowering, maturity)?
• Can dynamic satellite-tracked planting dates replace fixed calendar-based trigger windows to reduce basis risk?

4. Policyholder Enrollment, KYC & Geotagging

Metric: Digital KYC & Geotagging Adoption Potential (DKGAP)
• How are smallholder policyholders currently enrolled and onboarded (aggregators, MFBs, digital self-enrollment)?
• What percentage of insured farm plots have GPS-verified boundary polygon data versus rough centroid coordinates?
• What is the average operational onboarding cost per insured smallholder?

Insurance Protocol (Cont.) & Value Chain Bridging

Insurance Questionnaire: Claims, Reinsurance & Compliance

Completing Sections 5 through 9 for insurance risk syndication and claims automation.

5. In-Season Risk Monitoring & Accumulation

Metric: Claim Adjustment & Payout Efficiency Index (CAPEI)
• How do you track portfolio risk accumulation across contiguous geographical zones during the season?
• Do you maintain automated early warning mechanisms for drought, excessive rain, or vegetative depression before harvest?
• Could mid-season satellite anomaly alerts trigger pre-claim advisories to aggregators and lenders?

6. Claims Assessment, Ground-Truthing & CCE Costs

Metric: Reinsurance Treaty Readmission & Capacity Index (RTRCI)
• What is your operational process and expense structure for executing Crop Cut Experiments (CCEs) and manual loss adjustments?
• What is the average duration between trigger occurrence and final claim payout settlement to the policyholder?
• Can automated satellite yield verification substitute manual CCE field sampling by 70%+?

7. Payout Settlement Governance & Distribution

Metric: Supply Chain Trade Predictability & Intake Accuracy Index (SCTPIAI)
• What payment channels are used to distribute claim proceeds (mobile money, direct credit to lending institution, coop check-off account)?
• When policies are credit-linked, how are indemnities divided between debt settlement and direct cash payouts to farmers?
• What are the major reconciliation challenges and leakage risks during mass claim distribution?

8. Reinsurance Treaty Structuring, Cession & Capacity

Metric: Reinsurer acceptance feasibility; impact of ACIP datasets on reducing reinsurance rate-on-line (RoL)
• What proportion of your agricultural risk portfolio is ceded to international and regional reinsurers (e.g., Swiss Re, Africa Re, Kenya Re)?
• What are the core data prerequisites and audit trails mandated by reinsurers before underwriting parametric treaties in Kenya?
• Would independent satellite verification datasets lower reinsurance rate-on-line (RoL) and unlock expanded reinsurance capacity?

9. Regulatory Environment

Metric: Regulatory Compliance & Innovation Readiness Index (RCIRI)
• What are the Insurance Regulatory Authority (IRA) compliance requirements for filing new parametric or index-based insurance products under the Sandbox or standard filing framework?
• How do consumer protection guidelines mandate the disclosure of index parameters and basis risk disclaimers to smallholder farmers?
• What data localization, consent, and storage regulations govern the sharing of farmer agronomic and insurance loss data with external tech platforms?

Field Operations & Commercial Off-Take

Aggregator & Cooperative Market Validation Questionnaire

Structured inquiries covering off-taker operations, farmer check-offs, yield forecasting, side-selling control, and multi-party loan deductions.

1. Sourcing Model, Acreage Baseline & Intake

Metric: DSAP

• What is your active contracted farmer base, total under management acreage, and annual crop tonnage delivered across primary value chains?

• What percentage of smallholders operate under formalized outgrower schemes versus spot-market sourcing?

2. Production Forecasting & Trade Predictability

Metric: DSAP

• How do you currently estimate pre-harvest aggregate yields to commit forward commercial contracts to buyers/exporters?

• What is your historical variance percentage between pre-season estimated volume and actual gate delivery?

3. Input Pre-Financing & Working Capital Risk

Metric: DSAP

• Do you provide seed, fertilizer, or agrochemical advances to farmers? What is your current loss/default rate on seasonal input loans?

• How do you finance input books—balance sheet cash, commercial bank credit lines, or off-balance-sheet donor facilities?

4. In-Season Crop Health & Field Extension Opex

Metric: DSAP

• How many field extension officers do you deploy, and what is your annual expenditure on physical agronomist visits?

• Can remote sensing-based crop vegetative index tracking reduce your field agronomy operating expenditure?

5. Side-Selling Prevention & Yield Reconciliation

Metric: DSAP

• What percentage of expected yield is lost to side-selling to middlemen during harvest peak periods?

• Could parcel-level yield estimation tools alert management when delivered volumes deviate suspiciously from farm output?

6. Produce Intake, Digital Weighing & Settlement

Metric: DSAP

• Are produce collection centers equipped with digital scales integrated into an ERP, or are deliveries recorded manually on paper manifests?

• What is the average duration between farmgate weigh-in and farmer receipt of mobile money payout?

7. Multi-Party Split Deductions & Loan Recovery

Metric: DSAP

• Do you execute check-off split payments to recover third-party loans (MFBs, SACCOs, input providers) at the point of produce sale?

• What automated payment rails (e.g., VunaPay API, M-Pesa B2C) are currently deployed to execute simultaneous deductions?

8. Trading Commitments & ERP Integration

Metric: DSAP

• What Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) or farm management software (e.g., SAP, Farmforce, custom database) do you maintain?

• What pricing structure would your organization support for predictive crop monitoring: per-farmer fee, or basis points on handled tonnage?
