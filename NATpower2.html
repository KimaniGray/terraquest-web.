The Opportunity

This investment presents a unique investment opportunity in the Industrial Chemicals space, for any investor, featuring zero capital expenditure exposure to fixed infrastructure while offering direct exposure into both global markets and the Southern African market, specifically South Africa, Zambia, Mozambique, and Malawi. By leveraging an asset-backed, short-term trade deployment model, the facility captures acute regional supply-demand imbalances across Southern Africa. Through established tolling mechanics, robust physical collateral controls, and a senior secured waterfall framework, the transaction delivers an exceptionally attractive risk-adjusted yield while maintaining comprehensive downside protection—providing a seamless transition into the core investment structure and transaction flow detailed below.

Investment Structure

The transaction is organized as a revolving structured trade facility designed to finance the lifecycle conversion of bulk elemental sulphur into sulphuric acid.

Capital is deployed via a UAE SPV investment vehicle that holds title to feedstock and output, backed by statutory security and ring-fenced collection accounts.

The commercial operator manages upstream sourcing, logistics, and downstream sales, while processing is executed under an in-kind tolling agreement with an established refinery.

1. Market Situation

Historical Market & Current Supply

Southern Africa

Historical Market

The regional trade landscape for Sulphuric Acid in Southern Africa is defined by a severe structural supply deficit and acute price sensitivity. Benchmark historical pricing metrics from South Africa's leading domestic processor, Foskor (Pty) Ltd, illustrate a dramatic upward price inflection between Q1 2025 and Q2 2026, with spot prices advancing from R1,876/MT to R3,645/MT. When adjusted for foreign exchange movements—as the South African Rand appreciated from approximately 18.69 to 16.62 ZAR/USD—the effective USD clearing price more than doubled from ~$100/MT to $219/MT. This compounding cost escalation culminated in April 2026, when Foskor suspended public price listings, signaling an unprecedented tightening of uncontracted spot market inventory across the broader SADC region.

Current Supply

To satisfy core industrial and agricultural requirements across Southern Africa, South Africa maintains a continuous structural dependence on elemental Sulphur imports, consuming between 800,000 and 1,200,000 MT annually. While Foskor's flagship Richards Bay complex underpins domestic supply with an estimated annual output of 1,200,000 MT of Sulphuric Acid, the broader refining base remains highly consolidated among a select group of industrial chemical producers and base metal refiners, as detailed below:

1. Market Situation

Logistical Constraints & Regional Pricing

SADC Copperbelt

Cross-Border Bottlenecks

This concentrated domestic production base is further constrained by severe cross-border logistical bottlenecks across the SADC transit network. Stricter enforcement of dangerous goods transportation frameworks (notably SANS 10231/10232 mandates), complex hazmat permitting protocols, and restrictive operational limits on non-South African registered tankers have sharply reduced regional fleet availability. The resulting freight squeeze impedes cross-border hauliers from balancing supply imbalances, directly insulating regional price premiums across high-demand SADC mining hubs.

Copperbelt Distortion

Consequently, the SADC Copperbelt exhibits extreme pricing distortion and severe supply tightness. Following the introduction of export controls in April 2026, ex-works sulphuric acid pricing in Zambia spiked to $330/MT against domestic Zambian levels of ~$250/MT, while in the Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC), ex-works prices surged to $550/MT—representing the highest verified clearing level in the region. In acute localized shortage environments, port-stocked bagged lots have commanded extreme premiums up to $1,000/MT.

3. Risk Management

Four Primary Risk Vectors

Exposure & Mitigation

The TIGIN & Terraquest JV has isolated four primary risk vectors within the asset conversion cycle. Structural, legal, and operational safeguards have been embedded into the transaction documents to ring-fence the syndicate's principal.

Feedstock Rejection

The Exposure: ADNOC's Grade-A Sulphur baseline permits up to 500 ppm of Ash/Carbon, whereas Foskor's internal processing threshold mandates a strict 200 ppm maximum. A cargo clearing ADNOC parameters could theoretically be rejected upon arrival at the Foskor facility.

The Mitigation: The JV requires a binding bilateral "Foskor Addendum" as a strict Condition Precedent to drawdown. This addendum legally obligates Foskor to accept independent load-port certificates of quality/quantity issued at the ADNOC terminal, effectively removing localized rejection risk.

Asset Commingling

The Exposure: Raw Sulphur is delivered directly into Foskor's infrastructure, requiring the SPV to relinquish physical custody of the primary collateral during the conversion phase.

The Mitigation: The SPV retains absolute legal title to both the raw feedstock and the resulting 65% Customer Tonnage under a formalized bailment framework. The JV will mandate an independent Collateral Management Agent (e.g., SGS, Bureau Veritas) under a Tripartite Agreement to execute 24/7 surveillance and maintain physical lock-out controls over discharge manifolds.

Receivables Diversion

The Exposure: As the commercial originator, NATpower syndicates downstream offtake agreements directly. There is a structural risk of NATpower routing liquidations to non-syndicate accounts.

The Mitigation: No processed inventory leaves the Foskor perimeter without an authenticated Release Authorisation. This authorization is strictly issued by the SPV (acting as Security Agent) only after verified, cleared funds from the offtaker have settled in the SPV's ring-fenced Collection Account.

Demurrage Exposure

The Exposure: Under ADNOC CFR Discharge Instructions (Clause 20), bad weather delays after berthing count as full laytime. Additionally, delays caused by the SPV's shore equipment or shifting requirements are assessed to the SPV, potentially incurring demurrage penalties.

The Mitigation: A dedicated $20,000 per-shipment Demurrage Contingency has been embedded directly into the Capital Deployment Ledger. This absorbs unforeseen terminal or weather-related delays without eroding the baseline equity returns.

4. Waterfall Distributions

Equity & Security / Seniority Waterfall

Collection Account

Equity & Security. To underwrite the Capital Provider's exposure and incentivize Sponsor performance, the UAE SPV will hold equity warrants in NATpower Corporation PT. These warrants secure the SPV's collateral position against the downstream commercial operations and act as a hard performance backstop against any payment diversion or offtake default by the Sponsor. All liquidity events arising from the monetization of the 65% Customer Tonnage must clear exclusively through the ring-fenced Collection Account held by the UAE SPV. To enforce disciplined capital recovery, disbursements follow a strict seniority waterfall:

Tier 1

Principal

100% recovery of the principal deployed for upstream procurement, freight, insurance, and local execution per completed cycle.

Tier 2

Yield

Capital Partner: Receives 60% of the fixed base return (12% + Premium) generated on the deployed capital, alongside the 5.00% Arrangement Fee.

Investment Partner: Receives the remaining 40% of the fixed base return.

Tier 3

Taxes

Clearance of immediate statutory taxes, VAT, or sovereign duties levied on the product liquidation.

Tier 4

Opex

Reimbursement of verified, deferred operational expenditures incurred by the SPV or NATpower during the specific trade cycle (e.g., finalized PDA true-ups or verified CMA fees).

Tier 5

Carry

Capital Partner: Receives 30% of the performance carry on the net realized trading surplus.

Investment Partner: Receives the remaining 70% of the performance carry, serving as the management incentive for origination and asset structuring.

Tier 6

Residual

100% of all residual cash flow remaining in the Collection Account for the completed cycle sweeps to NATpower as the commercial sponsor.
