3. Take-Out Strategy

To provide a definitive, pre-structured exit for the syndicated lenders, we are structuring a 5-year Guaranteed Fund alongside a regulated investment bank acting as the Approved Issuer. The vehicle is structured to qualify strictly as an Approved Asset Class under Retirement Benefits Authority (RBA) guidelines, unlocking captive allocations from domestic pension funds, insurance companies, and SACCOs. At maturity, this vehicle executes a scheduled refinance of the syndicated facility, taking out the initial lender group in full while anchoring a long-term capital base to support subsequent tranches across the 5-year FRA mandate.

4. Guaranteed Asset Class

Under Kenya's statutory RBA guidelines (Table G, Category 4), retirement schemes are legally permitted to allocate up to 100% of their assets into Guaranteed Funds.

This structure transforms an unlisted senior-secured trade asset into a compliant institutional allocation. The investment bank, operating under CMA Cap. 485A, acts as the Approved Issuer, providing a primary contractual backstop that guarantees the preservation of 100% principal capital alongside a certified 6.00% net hard-currency (USD) minimum return. Pension trustees and investment committees bear zero mark-to-market, equity, or operational risk. The targeted integration of an Irrevocable Credit Wrap fundamentally upgrades the regulatory profile, classifying it as an "Admitted Asset" for insurers and unlocking an addressable KES 597 Billion institutional liquidity pool that is otherwise precluded from direct trade finance.

5. De-Risking Mechanisms

To deliver absolute capital protection for all deployed funds and eliminate execution risks for the syndicate, the asset structure relies on eight concurrent de-risking mechanisms:

1. Tax-Optimized Kenyan SPV

Capital is deployed exclusively into the TRIFIC SEZ SPV, creating a bankruptcy-remote firewall. The SEZ domiciliation provides a 10-year corporate tax exemption and strictly ring-fences the assets from the operational or sovereign liabilities of the FRA. Furthermore, the SPV is 100% legally and beneficially owned by an independent CMA-licensed Corporate Trustee to ensure absolute investor protection.

2. Perfected True Sale Security

The facility advances zero unsecured credit. It executes a "True Sale" Master Commodity Repurchase Agreement, acquiring unencumbered legal title to the commodity. Cash is only advanced after registering a comprehensive fixed and floating debenture, encompassing the 10% cash escrow, primary collection accounts, and statutory Negotiable Warehouse Receipts (WHRs).

3. Controlled DvP Cash Deployment

Cash is deployed strictly via MT103 on a 'Delivery vs. Payment' (DvP) basis. Capital is released solely after an internationally certified Collateral Manager (backed by a mandatory $8M Professional Indemnity policy) verifies the physical grain and lodges the WHRs with the Document Custodian.

4. Day-One Over-Collateralization

Risk is neutralized at inception through aggressive volumetric discounting, instantly establishing a mandatory 150% Asset Value Floor. Daily independent price verification ensures physical asset coverage securely absorbs any localized price volatility.

5. 10% Pre-Funded Liquidity Reserve

A 10% cash security is deducted upfront from all advanced funds and locked in a Trustee-controlled Margin Escrow Account. This provides an immediate first-loss liquidity buffer that perfectly bridges the deductible threshold of the Collateral Manager's insurance coverage.

6. Comprehensive Insurance Wrap

The SPV directly appoints tier-1 underwriters to secure the assets. This includes a Multi-Peril property wrap (covering fire, flood, and theft) and a targeted Political Risk Insurance (PRI) policy that explicitly neutralizes sovereign interference, export embargoes, and Breach of Contract.

7. Sovereign Waivers & Export Permits

Financial close is strictly conditional upon the upfront issuance of a Master Export Permit (exempting the grain from future embargoes), comprehensive tax exemptions from the ZRA, and absolute, irrevocable waivers of sovereign immunity from the Zambian state.

8. Automated Secondary Liquidation

Should the obligor default, the Security Trustee bypasses traditional judicial delays and executes an automated T-Zero to T+15 liquidation timeline. Leveraging the Master Export Permits, pre-staged commodities are immediately redirected to cash-ready regional buyers (DRC, Angola, Kenya), executing strictly in US Dollars to immediately make the Noteholders whole.
