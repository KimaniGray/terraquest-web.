3. Take-Out Strategy

The exit and scaling architecture operates through a disciplined bridge-to-institutional take-out mechanism:

  • Stage 1 (Agri Harvest Bridge): Agri Harvest provides immediate, senior-secured liquidity to meet the Food Reserve Agency's (FRA) procurement window, collateralized day-one against physical warehouse receipts and exchequer flows.
  • Stage 2 (Local Institutional Take-Out): Concurrently, TerraQuest, RMB, and their consortium partners co-architect a localized, 5-year Guaranteed Fund vehicle approved under Kenyan Retirement Benefits Authority (RBA) guidelines. Because RBA Table G (Category 4) permits domestic retirement schemes to allocate up to 100% of their assets into Guaranteed Funds, this vehicle unlocks an addressable KES 597 Billion institutional liquidity pool.
  • The Long-Term Asset: At maturity, this newly established East African vehicle executes a scheduled refinance of the Agri Harvest bridge facility, taking out the initial capital in full. This establishes a permanent, co-managed East African agricultural securitisation platform capable of expanding beyond Zambia into equivalent statutory reserves and agricultural corporates across the region (such as NFRA in Tanzania and GMB in Zimbabwe).

5. De-Risking Mechanisms

To deliver absolute capital protection for all deployed funds and eliminate execution risks for the syndicate, the asset structure relies on eight concurrent de-risking mechanisms:

1. Tax-Optimized Kenyan SPV

Capital is deployed exclusively into the TRIFIC SEZ SPV, creating a bankruptcy-remote firewall. The SEZ domiciliation provides a 10-year corporate tax exemption and strictly ring-fences the assets from the operational or sovereign liabilities of the FRA.

2. Perfected True Sale Security

The facility advances zero unsecured credit. It executes a "True Sale" Master Commodity Repurchase Agreement, acquiring unencumbered legal title to the commodity.

3. Controlled DvP Cash Deployment

Cash is deployed strictly via MT103 on a 'Delivery vs. Payment' (DvP) basis. Capital is released solely after an internationally certified Collateral Manager verifies the physical grain and lodges the WHRs with the Document Custodian.

4. Day-One Over-Collateralization

Risk is neutralized at inception through aggressive volumetric discounting, instantly establishing a mandatory 150% Asset Value Floor.

5. 10% Pre-Funded Liquidity Reserve

A 10% cash security is deducted upfront from all advanced funds and locked in a Trustee-controlled Margin Escrow Account.

6. Comprehensive Insurance Wrap

The SPV directly appoints tier-1 underwriters to secure the assets, including a Multi-Peril property wrap and a targeted Political Risk Insurance (PRI) policy.

7. Sovereign Waivers & Export Permits

Financial close is strictly conditional upon the upfront issuance of a Master Export Permit (exempting the grain from future embargoes), comprehensive tax exemptions from the ZRA, and absolute, irrevocable waivers of sovereign immunity from the Zambian state.

8. Automated Secondary Liquidation

Should the obligor default, the Security Trustee bypasses traditional judicial delays and executes an automated T-Zero to T+15 liquidation timeline. Pre-staged commodities are immediately redirected to cash-ready regional buyers, executing strictly in US Dollars.

6. Agri Harvest Alignment

The TRFF-1 facility has been intentionally engineered to plug directly into RMB's Agri Harvest Programme infrastructure. By mirroring the Agri Harvest fiduciary and constitutional mechanics, TRFF-1 provides RMB with a high-yielding, fully compliant asset ready for immediate institutional deployment.

Multi-Seller Fit

Agri Harvest is structured as an umbrella Multi-Seller and Servicer Programme designed for agricultural market players to place farmer debtor securitisation transactions into capital markets. Under TRFF-1, the FRA functions as the anchor "Seller" and "Servicer" of a dedicated Transaction, packaging farmer procurement advances and sovereign receivables into the exact securitisation format Agri Harvest was built to house.

Eligible Asset

TRFF-1 qualifies under Clause 2 of the Agri Harvest Programme Memorandum as an agricultural financing facility and receivable funding farmer procurement, with the physical grain and Negotiable Warehouse Receipts (WHRs) serving as perfected collateral.

SBL Exemption

Traditional commercial banks face single-borrower concentration caps when lending directly to statutory entities. By executing an off-balance-sheet "True Sale" into a ring-fenced SPV, TRFF-1 allows Agri Harvest and its participating institutional funders to deploy scale capital unconstrained by commercial bank SBLs.

USD Flexibility

The Agri Harvest legal framework accommodates issuances in any designated "Specified Currency." TRFF-1 leverages this flexibility to deploy a hard-currency (USD) tranche, matching the USD cash flows of regional export contracts while eliminating domestic currency devaluation risk.

Fiduciary Match

Agri Harvest relies on a dedicated Security SPV to issue limited-recourse guarantees backed by asset pledges. TRFF-1 mirrors this fiduciary firewall through its independent CMA-regulated Security Trustee (MTC Trust), which holds the all-asset debenture, warehouse receipts, and cash escrow accounts, ensuring frictionless integration into RMB's risk and compliance matrix.
