PlayformIQ Strategy
01 — Creative Brief

The "Beat The Creator" Hook

The campaign does not begin with a standard commercial. It begins with @mediamk issuing a personal challenge to his followers.

❌ The Old Model

BMW → Creator → Sponsored Reel → View

Passive consumption. The viewer scrolls past after a few seconds without absorbing key vehicle specifications or technology.

⚡ The PlayformIQ Model

BMW → indaHash → Creator → Challenge → Game → Score → Viral Share

The audience is not asked to watch an advert; they are asked to prove their knowledge by beating @mediamk's score of 1,850.

📋 Brief Direction

“Don't make this look like a BMW commercial. Make it feel like your audience is challenging you.”
